Detective's Rules of Investigation

Determine which of the five suspects is the killer. Apply backward chaining in your investigation.

R1

if suspect has means

and suspect has opportunity

and suspect has motive

then suspect is killer

R2

if suspect is at crime scene

and time is within time of death

then suspect has opportunity

R3

if knife has victim's blood

and cause of death is stabbing

then knife is murder weapon

R4

if bullet found at crime scene

and cause of death is bullet wound

then pistol is murder weapon

R5

if victim drank poisoned water

and cause of death is poisoning

then murder weapon is poison

R6

if shovel has victim's blood

and cause of death is stabbing

then shovel is murder weapon

R7

if suspect held murder weapon

then suspect has means

R8

if suspect was angry at victim

or suspect gain financial benefits

then suspect has motive
